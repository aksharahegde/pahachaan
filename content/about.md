## Akshara Hegde

I'm Akshara Hegde, a full-stack engineer and open-source contributor. I build products, developer tools, and AI-powered apps, mostly in public so the bugs have witnesses. Outside of shipping code, I write short stories and essays about systems, productivity, and why the thing that should've taken an hour took a week.

This site, [akshara.dev](https://akshara.dev), is my personal portfolio, blog, and changelog. It's also a free, open-source [Nuxt](https://nuxt.com) template — anyone can fork it and run their own content-driven portfolio on top of [Nuxt Content](https://content.nuxt.com), [Nuxt Studio](https://nuxt.studio), and [Nuxt UI](https://ui.nuxt.com).

### What I work on

- **Developer tools** — CLIs, scrapers, and utilities like [Crawlboy](https://crawlboy.aksharahegde.xyz) and [ThreatPkg](/blog/tracking-compromised-packages-threatpkg), built to solve problems I ran into myself.
- **Open source** — maintaining and contributing to projects across the Nuxt, Vue, and Python ecosystems. See [Projects](/projects) for the full list.
- **Writing** — implementation notes, retrospectives, and essays on the [blog](/blog), syndicated to [Ink](https://ink.aksharahegde.xyz).
- **AI-native workflows** — Claude Skills, MCP, and agent tooling, documented on the [Resources](/resources) page.

### How this site is built

The stack is Nuxt 4, Nuxt Content, Nuxt UI, and Tailwind CSS, deployed as a static/edge-rendered site. Design tokens and UI guidelines are published as a machine-readable [DESIGN.md](/DESIGN.md), and the site exposes an [llms.txt](/llms.txt) so AI agents can understand what it is and when to use it without scraping the whole thing.

The fastest way to reach me is listed on the [Contact](/contact) page — email, a booking link, and social profiles. I'm generally open to collaborating on open-source projects, technical writing, and interesting engineering problems.
