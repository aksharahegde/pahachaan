## Privacy

This is a personal portfolio and blog. It collects very little, and none of it is sold or shared with third parties beyond the processors listed below.

### Analytics

The site uses [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused, cookieless analytics tool, to understand aggregate traffic — page views, referrers, and rough geography. Umami does not use tracking cookies and does not build cross-site profiles of visitors.

### Live visitor presence

The homepage shows a live count of other people currently browsing the site, powered by [Ably](https://ably.com). To place a visitor as an approximate dot on the map, the server reads coarse, IP-derived location headers (city-level latitude/longitude) provided by the hosting edge network — no precise location, device fingerprint, or personal identifier is requested or stored. Each visitor is assigned a random, session-scoped client ID that is discarded when they leave; it is not tied to an account or stored after the session ends.

### Contact data

If you email me or book a call via [Cal.com](https://cal.com/aksharahegde), I use whatever you send only to reply to you. I don't add contacts to a mailing list without asking first.

### Cookies

This site does not set advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Theme preference (light/dark/color settings) is stored locally in your browser via `localStorage`, not as a cookie, and never leaves your device.

### Third-party links

Project links, blog posts, and the [resources](/resources) page link out to external sites (GitHub, npm, other blogs, tools). Once you leave akshara.dev, that destination's own privacy policy applies.

### Changes
